Designed strictly around the workflow of solo creators, freelancers, and agile consultants.

Horizontal Gantt Timeline

Continuous swimlanes grouped by Project and Client. Clearly spot delivery overlaps, adjust deadlines, and visualize your entire roadmap across weeks and months without messy tables.

24-Hour Hourly Calendar

Time-block your actual day with an anti-overlap hourly scheduler. Simultaneously scheduled jobs are dynamically shifted side-by-side, color-coded by your project's accent.

Native Rich Markdown

Ditch plain raw text. Document bugs, PR details, and specifications with hierarchical headers, interactive checklists - [ ], monospace syntax blocks, and instant Live Preview.

Paste Screenshots (⌘V)

Hit ⌘V directly in any task to attach problem screenshots from your clipboard. Attach links to Figma, GitHub pull requests, or design files with responsive thumbnail previews.

Kanban & 1-Click Status Reports

Organize execution with To Do, In Progress, Review, and Completed columns. Generate a ready-to-copy formatted status report to send to your clients in seconds.

Meeting Notes & Decision Q&A

Store meeting agendas, video call URLs (Google Meet, Zoom, Teams), and a structured Questions & Answers log so commitments and decisions are never lost.

Global Quick Search (⌘K)

Jump to any task, project, client, or meeting minute instantaneously with Spotlight-like speed. Navigate your entire workload purely from the keyboard.

Apple iCloud & SwiftData

Fast native SQLite database backed by automatic Apple CloudKit synchronization. Works flawlessly offline, and syncs across all your Macs under your personal Apple ID.

Bilingual (EN / PT-BR)

Seamlessly toggle between English and Brazilian Portuguese directly from the sidebar footer. Automatic macOS system language detection included.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Are there any hidden monthly fees or subscriptions?

None. Worklane is a traditional, honest one-time purchase on the Mac App Store. Once you buy it, you own it forever with no recurring bills.

How does iCloud synchronization work?

Worklane uses Apple's official CloudKit framework integrated into SwiftData. Your tasks, clients, and timelines are mirrored securely to your private iCloud container (iCloud.br.com.prodit.worklane). We do not host external databases and have no access to your files.

What are the system requirements?

Worklane requires a Mac running macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or macOS 15.0 (Sequoia) or later. It runs natively on both Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and Intel Macs.

Can I use Worklane offline?

Yes, absolutely. Because Worklane uses a local SQLite database, you can create, edit, and organize all your projects and tasks without any internet connection. As soon as your Mac reconnects, iCloud replicates changes automatically.
